Due diligence

Do your homework before you invest

Angel investors back ideas at the very earliest stage. This round is about solving problems, not building a company: no business model, no TAM, no revenue plan.

01 · Pain point

Is the pain point real?

Has someone you know actually hit it? Specifics win.

02 · Solution

Does it actually solve it?

With this project, does the annoying thing get simpler?

03 · MVP

Does the MVP work today?

Live beats slides. Small and finished beats big and half-built.

04 · Edge

What’s the edge?

An angle, approach, or detail you didn’t see coming.
